3. How Your Data Is Stored and Protected

  • Cloud Storage (Google Firebase): All business firms, bills, quotations, and catalog items are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore under your unique authenticated user account (users/{userId}/...).
  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest on Google Cloud servers.
  • Strict Access Control: Firestore Security Rules enforce owner-only access, ensuring only the signed-in Google account owner can read, create, edit, or delete their billing data.
  • Local PDF Caching: Generated A4 PDF invoices and quotations are temporarily rendered in the app's private cache directory solely so you can preview, print, save via Android's document picker, or share them via your chosen app.

4. Permissions Used by the App

  • INTERNET: Used exclusively for Google Sign-In authentication and real-time synchronization with Cloud Firestore.
  • Zero Broad Storage Permissions: The app does not request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or READ_MEDIA_* permissions. Saving PDFs uses Android's zero-permission Storage Access Framework (SAF), and sharing PDFs uses a scoped FileProvider.

5. Third-Party Services

GST Bill uses the following trusted Google services for core functionality:

  • Google Sign-In / Credential Manager & Firebase Authentication — for secure sign-in.
  • Google Cloud Firestore — for secure, real-time cloud storage of your billing ledger.

For details on how Google handles infrastructure and authentication data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy & Security documentation.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your business records remain stored in your cloud account only as long as you keep your account active. You have full control over your data:

  • Delete Individual Records: You can delete any firm, bill, quotation, or catalog item directly inside the app at any time.
  • Delete Entire Account & All Cloud Data (In-App): Open GST Bill → go to the Firms tab → scroll to Cloud Storage & Legal → tap Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm. This permanently deletes your firms, invoices, quotations, catalog items, and profile records from Cloud Firestore and removes your app account.
  • Delete Account via Email Request: If you cannot access the app, email Ashvanshakti@gmail.com from your registered Google email address with the subject "Delete GST Bill Account". All associated cloud records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.
